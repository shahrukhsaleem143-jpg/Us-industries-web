Product Development / 02

From idea to reality.

  2. 02
  3. 03

  1. 01

    Concept

    It starts with your tech pack, sketch or reference garment. We review construction, fabric and target cost before anything is cut.

  2. 02

    Material

    Fabric, weight, composition, trims and colour are chosen against the brief: jersey, fleece, French terry, thermal and rib, from 160 to 450 GSM.

  3. 03

    Development

    Patterns are made, artwork is separated and embellishment is engineered: print, embroidery, applique, stones and wash.

  4. 04

    Sampling

    A dedicated sampling room builds fit and development samples, and turns comments around quickly.

  5. 05

    Approval

    Fit, measurements, shade and finish are signed off on the pre-production sample. That sample becomes the standard for bulk.

  6. 06

    Production

    Bulk moves through cutting, sewing, embellishment and finishing, with inline checks at every stage.

Quality / 06

Quality is not a final check. It's a process.

Quality is built in at every stage, not added at the end. Each order runs against customer-specific QA requirements, measurements and shade control.

  1. 01

    Fabric inspection

    Weight, shade, hand-feel and defects are checked on arrival.

  2. 02

    Pattern & cutting accuracy

    Markers and cut panels are verified against the pattern.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Seam quality, stitch density and tension, checked inline.

  4. 04

    Measurements

    Every size is measured against the approved spec.

  5. 05

    Print & embroidery

    Placement, registration, colour and durability.

  6. 06

    Applique

    Edge finish, positioning and bond.

  7. 07

    Washing

    Shade continuity and hand-feel matched to the standard.

  8. 08

    Finishing

    Trimming, pressing and labelling.

  9. 09

    Final inspection

    Random AQL inspection before release.

  10. 10

    Packing

    Correct ratios, stickers and carton marks.

BSCI certified manufacturing  ·  Needle control and metal detection when required
