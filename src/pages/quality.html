Quality

Quality is a process.

Not a final check. Quality is built into every stage, from the fabric roll to the export carton.

Checkpoints

Ten points of control.

  1. 01

    Fabric inspection

    Weight, width, shade, hand-feel and defects checked before anything is cut.

  2. 02

    Pattern & cutting

    Markers and cut panels verified against the approved pattern.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Seam quality, stitch density and thread tension checked on the line.

  4. 04

    Measurements

    Graded sizes measured against your spec and tolerances.

  5. 05

    Print & embroidery

    Placement, registration, colour and wash durability.

  6. 06

    Applique

    Edge finish, positioning and bond strength.

  7. 07

    Washing

    Shade control and hand-feel matched to the approved standard.

  8. 08

    Finishing

    Trimming, pressing, labelling and presentation.

  9. 09

    Final inspection

    Random final inspection to AQL before release.

  10. 10

    Packing

    Correct ratios, stickers, polybags and carton marks.
