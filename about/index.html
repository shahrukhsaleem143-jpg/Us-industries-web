About

Made with purpose.

An apparel manufacturing and export company built around one idea: the product comes first.

We are makers for brands that care how things are made.

US Industries develops and manufactures premium knitwear, fleece and streetwear for fashion labels, retailers and private-label programmes. Development, sampling, cutting, sewing, printing, embroidery and finishing all happen under one roof in Karachi.

Our roots are in four decades of garment-making. Today that experience serves a simple goal: build the right product, then build it the same way every time.

What we believe

Three things we hold to.

  1. 01

    Development is the work.

    Great production starts long before bulk. We put real engineering into fit, fabric and embellishment at the sample stage, so that bulk is a repeat, not a gamble.

  2. 02

    Consistency is respect.

    Your customer doesn't see our process. They see the hundredth piece. It should feel exactly like the first.

  3. 03

    Relationships outlast orders.

    We plan for the next season with you, not just this one. Straight answers and steady communication make that possible.

Made in Karachi. Close to you in Texas.
